Good to know

Donation FAQ.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Receipts are issued for every gift. Deductibility depends on your country of residence, we're glad to provide documentation your tax authority may require.

How do I know the money is used well?

Our accounts are independently audited each year and published on the Governance page, alongside our annual report. Around 82% of spending goes directly to field programs.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Choose “Monthly” above to set up a recurring gift, predictable support helps us plan programs that last.

What payment methods can I use?

Mobile Money, bank transfer, and card. In the live site, choosing an amount opens a secure checkout for the method you prefer.

Can I give to a specific program?

Yes, you can direct your gift to agriculture, water, energy, or livelihoods. Tell us in the checkout note, or ask NOVA AI for guidance.

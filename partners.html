Partner types

Four kinds of partnership.

Each relationship plays a distinct role in making results last.

Government

National ministries and district local governments that embed our work in public systems and policy.

Development partners

UN agencies and climate funds that bring technical depth and co-financing.

Community

Cooperatives, savings groups, and farmer associations who lead delivery on the ground.

Private sector

Businesses supplying solar, seed, and markets, and funding through CSR.

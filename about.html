Who we are

A Ugandan foundation for a resilient green economy.

NOVA exists to strengthen the institutions and the social-economic systems that make green, climate-resilient growth possible, and to do it with integrity the people we serve can see.

Our foundation
“Akwata empola atuuka wala.”Luganda proverb: the one who moves steadily goes far. It guides how we build, patiently, and to last.

Our Vision

A foundation built on the ethical principles of professionalism, integrity, transparency, and accountable leadership.

Our Mission

To support the strengthening of institutional and social-economic systems geared toward sustainable and resilient green economic growth across the country.

What we stand for

Four values, held in practice.

Values only matter when they shape decisions. Ours show up in how we budget, report, and partner.

Integrity

We do what we said we would, and we account for the difference when we don't.

Transparency

Our finances are audited and published. Anyone can see where the money goes.

Community first

Programs are designed with communities, not for them, and led locally.

Sustainability

We measure success by what lasts after the project closes, not activity counts.

Our story

From an idea to six districts.

2021

NOVA is registered

Founded in Kampala as a non-profit focused on the green economy, and registered as NGO No. NB/2021/00482.

2022

First field programs

Climate-smart agriculture and WASH pilots launch in Wakiso and Mukono with community cooperatives.

2023

Energy & livelihoods

Solar reaches rural schools and clinics; women-led savings groups begin green enterprises.

2024

Six districts, first audit published

Programs expand to Mbale, Gulu, Jinja and Kasese; the first audited statements and annual report go public.

2025

Institutional strengthening

Partnerships with district local governments deepen to make results last beyond grant cycles.
